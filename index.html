Compliance notes

Disclaimer: this app is not a certified Electronic Logging Device (ELD). It may not satisfy the legal logbook requirements in your state, province, or country — check your local laws before relying on it as a record of duty status. No guarantee of legal compliance is made; use is at your own risk.

Canada: this app is built as an electronic recording device under s.83 of the federal Hours of Service Regulations — it records the same information as the paper daily log, keeps the previous 14 days viewable on screen, prints on demand, and tracks your cycle hours. You must still: sign each printed daily log, keep blank paper log forms in the truck (your carrier provides them), and be able to show the last 14 days if an inspector asks.

United States: a phone app is not a US FMCSA-registered ELD. If you drive in the United States, you need a registered ELD — this app alone does not satisfy US rules.

Limits shown are a guide only. You are responsible for staying within the rules.
